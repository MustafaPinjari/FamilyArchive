## Platform

## Users
- **Wider Community & Relatives**: Exploring ancestral heritage, genealogical branches, family elders, and multi-generational historical lineage in a dignified public-facing showcase.
- **Immediate Family Members**: Accessing their individual verified Digital Member ID Cards, kinship connections, and personal identity records.
- **Family Leads & Administrators**: Managing real historical records, legal deeds, identity proofs, Google Drive sync, and relationship linkages.

## Product Purpose
The **Pinjari Heritage Archive** ("PINJAR BADDA") serves as the definitive ancestral registry and living digital heritage archive for the Pinjari family. It honors the lineage beginning with Patriarch Mohammad Pinjari, unifies family history across generations, preserves authoritative family records, and equips members with permanent, verifiable digital membership credentials.

## Positioning
Unlike generic genealogy platforms (Ancestry/MyHeritage) or plain cloud storage, the Pinjari Heritage Archive merges deep cultural identity, verified genealogical kinship trees, and an authoritative document vault into a bespoke, heirloom-grade digital archive.

## Operating Context
- **Semi-Private Security Model**:
  - **Public / Shared Layer**: Interactive genealogical family tree, elder profiles, lineage connections, and heritage pride ("Ek Khuda Ek Parivar").
  - **Restricted Vault Layer**: Protected identity documents, legal deeds, civil certificates, and financial papers accessible only with authorized credentials or authenticated session tokens.
- **Digital & Physical Bridges**: Official ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 Heritage Member ID Cards equipped with permanent QR codes that connect physical cards directly to digital document cupboards.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Zero Mock / Demo Data**: All displayed records, photos, documents, and relationships must be authentic family data imported via Admin / Google Drive sync or entered by family administrators.
- **Dual Export Formats**: High-resolution 2-page print-ready color PDF export and standalone print-optimized views immune to browser background color stripping.
- **Nested & Multi-Generational Kinship**: Accurate lineage chains, spousal records, and nested descendant trees across generations.
- **Bulk Operations**: Bulk download (ZIP) and document preview for vault materials.

## Evidence on Hand
- Authoritative SQLite database (`data/archive.db`) tracking active marriages, verified parent-child relationships, and genuine family documents.
- Active Google Drive integration architecture for synchronized document storage.

## Product Principles
1. **Dignity & Authenticity**: Treat family history with archival reverence. Never fabricate data, generate synthetic filler, or alter historical names.
2. **Semi-Private Boundary by Design**: Keep public heritage open and inspiring while rigorously shielding personal civil documents and sensitive papers behind authorized controls.
3. **Multi-Generational Clarity**: Design with utmost legibility and intuitive navigation for elders, working adults on mobile phones, and youth exploring their ancestry.
4. **Heirloom Longevity**: Ensure identity cards, QR links, and exported PDFs remain permanent, stable, and visually pristine across digital devices and physical prints.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Multilingual accessibility with Hindi/Urdu transliteration support for names.
- High-contrast visual elements, legible serif and sans typography, and print-color adjust permanence.
